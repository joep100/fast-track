This page is nothing to do with Cyclone

Different colours, different fonts, different everything — which is the point. The tracking widget is one line of script and it changes nothing about the page it sits on, because it lives in its own shadow root where this page’s CSS cannot reach it.

Two ways to open it

The button in the bottom right, which the script adds on its own.

Or your own link anywhere on the page: track a delivery.

Numbers to try

11097627 delivered · 11097628 out for delivery · 11097629 collected · 11097630 on the way · 11097631 booked · 11097632 failed. Anything else gives a miss.

A link can also open it straight onto a job, which is what would go in the text message: add ?track=11097627 to the address.
